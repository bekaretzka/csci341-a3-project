Research Conference and Paper Review Management System

CSCI 341, Assignment 3. Use the menu to manage the data:

  • Users: authors and reviewers, with their profiles
  • Papers: authors, keywords and versions
  • Reviews: a reviewer cannot review a paper they wrote
  • Sessions: and scheduling accepted papers into them
  • API docs: the same operations as an interactive API page

Conferences, tracks and reviewer pools are filled by the seed script and are read-only. A rejected change shows a red message and nothing is saved.
